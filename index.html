Research

I discover, explain, model, and apply the uniquely human processes of judgment and decision making.

I study consumers in social contexts, aiming to reverse-engineer the cognitive processes underlying their decisions. My research focuses on prosocial behavior, freedom of choice, luxury consumption, and other forms of social preference–based decision making. I use behavioral experiments, computational cognitive modeling, and large language models to investigate these questions.
